# v0.9.2 retained-versus-final case variation

The compressed [paired-row CSV](v0.9.2-case-variation.csv.gz) retains every
old/final outcome, classification, configuration and analyzer version, keyed by
report adapter and case ID. The [summary](v0.9.2-case-variation-summary.json)
binds its compressed and uncompressed SHA-256 digests and the source selection.

The baseline is the actual published v0.9.0 freeze, not the unpublished v0.9.1
preparation. The paired population is 4,252 report rows in 92 partitions across
1,108 distinct fixtures. Smoke duplication and unscored calibration are
retained; these row counts are not pooled analyzer rates. Published rates must
come from the validated freeze's generated scorecards. Profiles and tiers stay
separate. Different analyzer/configuration versions prevent causal attribution.

## Changes requiring interpretation

Bifrost has four newly correct decisive answers and one newly wrong decisive
answer, a known C# false positive, relative to v0.9.0. All other paired outcomes
remain unchanged. This is improvement on four assertions, not a claim of full
policy qualification or soundness. Native catalog absence remains unsupported,
and incomplete discovery remains inconclusive.

CodeQL's three native profiles retain 36 runner errors, twelve each for Java,
JavaScript and Python. Their retained stderr reports that the pinned
`codeql/*-queries@version:codeql-suites/*-security-extended.qls` query pack cannot
be found. This is a benchmark/runtime-pack lookup failure, not an analyzer
false negative or an unsupported-language finding. The earlier Swift runner
error remains separate. One other formerly decisive cell becomes inconclusive;
three answers become wrong and one becomes correct. Configuration and adapter
changes limit attribution to the analyzer release alone.

Joern has 62 formerly decisive Rust core rows now inconclusive (31 positive
and 31 negative assertions). Missing endpoint or
activation/resource proof cannot be upgraded from an empty or superficially
favorable native result. The final 108 Swift rows remain 96 inconclusive and
12 unsupported, with semantic-only qualification and no certified timing.
Other Joern slices retain their independent typed outcomes.

FlowDroid, Infer, OpenTaint, Pysa and Semgrep outcomes are unchanged on their
paired rows. Stable unsupported and inconclusive outcomes remain explicit
coverage limits. No missing result is invented, no denominator excluded to
improve a score, and no original ledger/report is replaced.
