common-108 group in fresh campaign `20261122-06`. It supplements A28 and A30;
the frozen v0.9.2 release and all earlier plans, reports and raw evidence remain

user was asked whether to preregister a fresh diagnostic continuation starting

contention flags and no settled-timing certification. The user answered:

unchanged repository `v092_timing_campaign.Observer` to prove quiescent MCP/LSP

dispositions, exact analyzer/runtime/query/configuration identities, fresh
diagnostic controls and strict frozen outcome/diagnostic/checkpoint comparison
remain unchanged. Use new source, attempt and evidence paths only. Retain all

This remains unqualified diagnostic timing evidence. Original settled A24
qualification, full model qualification, scored activation, cold/warm
