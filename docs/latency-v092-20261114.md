# Prospective v0.9.2 timing campaign — 14 November 2026

This supplement is registered before analyzer dispatch. The earliest launch is
02:00 Africa/Johannesburg on 14 November 2026 (`2026-11-14T00:00:00Z`), conditional
on readiness. It does not amend the frozen v0.9.2 correctness release at
`86092336d8f9ca32fe9e2c4042c2dced76c1feb1`, any existing report, or earlier timing
attempt. Preparation and every new attempt have separate append-only paths.

## Admission and identity

The campaign retains all 84 cold groups and their case/report membership from
the release execution machinery, using the final v0.9.2 analyzer selection.
At least one eligible cold group from each of the eight analyzer families must
be ready before launch. Missing language-specific dependencies remain blocked
even when another group for that analyzer is ready. Restoring a CLI alone does
not restore a custom query pack, compiler, model, or extractor.

`reports/timing/v0.9.2/20261114-01/qualified-plan.json` binds the prospective
runtime bindings, source projection, executable digests, full runtime manifests,
group arguments, environment, timeouts, queue order, and helper digests. The
controller verifies committed bytes before dispatch. Relocated runtime trees
retain derivation receipts against the frozen inventories; any materialized
venv metadata or launcher change has its own explicit prospective identity.
The pinned JDK's real executables replace changed macOS Java dispatcher stubs
through explicit arguments, rather than silently relying on `JAVA_HOME`.

Builds, dependency recovery, source materialization, hashing, version witnesses,
and fixture setup remain outside the adapter's measured subprocess boundaries.
No analyzer measurements are authorized during daytime preparation.

## Quiet windows and uninterrupted commands

Every ready command group requires a new observed window of at least 300
monotonic seconds with at least 90% aggregate CPU idle. Samples must be no more
than 60 seconds apart, with no competing analyzer, build, benchmark, or other
significant CPU/I/O workload. The existing narrowly defined inactive MCP/LSP
service treatment is retained. Missing observations reset the quiet window.
Polling waits are bounded to at most 60 seconds.

Once a command starts, background CPU activity, Mail/Contacts syncing, and
missing CPU observations are recorded without interrupting it or stopping the
queue. After completion, the controller waits for a new quiet window before
dispatching another group. A contended or unknown interval remains unqualified
as uncontended evidence. No unrelated process is stopped or killed.

The unchanged genuine command-failure, resource, storage, provenance, serial
ownership and per-command deadline gates still apply. Cleanup is restricted to
identity-proven owned processes. There is no automatic retry after a genuine
failure. The bounded 48-hour campaign window is checked between commands and
while waiting for quiet; it does not impose a new interruption on an active
command. The complete queue can extend beyond one night; unexecuted groups are
reported explicitly if the wall window ends.

## Measurements and acceptance

Cold observations retain each unchanged per-case invocation and every missing
duration, with per-group distributions and denominators. They describe different
cases, not repeated-trial variance. Unsupported, inconclusive and runner-error
states remain visible. Duration alone never certifies successful analysis.

The supported Joern/Java warm arm uses A15/A21 prefix batches `1,2,4,8,16`.
Semgrep/Java uses `1,2,4,8,12`: static inspection of the frozen fixtures yields
12 cases with identical resolved rule text, so the earlier pending 16-case
proposal could not execute. This prospective correction uses no new analyzer
observations. Both arms retain two complete repeats, all batches and their
equivalence restrictions. Other warm arms remain explicitly unsupported.
The A24 overhead arms retain their fixed languages and three repeats per arm,
including both Joern/PHP and Joern/Java. They are separately labelled upper-bound
estimates over trivial fixtures; no overhead is subtracted from cold timings.
Every auxiliary arm must independently satisfy its runtime and quiet gates.
The frozen FlowDroid overhead witness names the analyzer jar and Android
platform; its cold kernel identity additionally names D8 materialization.
Auxiliary identity validation preserves that existing distinction, while the
new runtime manifest and explicit arguments bind D8 separately.

The first eight cold groups cover one preregistered eligible group per analyzer;
the remaining cold groups retain the frozen relative order, followed by the
registered auxiliary arms. This prevents the queue from spending its entire
initial frontier on a single analyzer. No result-dependent reordering or retry
is allowed.

Each cold report is reconciled with the immutable release for population,
tool/version/build identity, fixture/configuration/profile and outcomes. A
semantic difference is retained and blocks acceptance; it is not normalized
away. Completed commands remain candidate evidence until independent report,
provenance, environment and publication review passes. The runner's quiet flag
is a limited internal predicate, not external certification. Existing PID/start
polling does not prove complete process-descendant containment or complete I/O
attribution; those limitations stay separate and visible.

## Aggregated display

The overview shows all eight analyzers in frozen selection order. For a
descriptive whole-corpus row, it pools raw per-case wall times, rather than
averaging group medians, and labels the mixed languages, tiers, populations and
profiles. It reports captured/planned groups and timed/report-row denominators.
Missing observations never become zero. Per-slice values remain available.
This display is not a matched population comparison, performance ranking, or
cross-release speed claim. Its use for the earlier 13 November capture is
retrospective; this document preregisters it only for the new campaign.

New results are published as a separately versioned timing supplement after
review. Earlier evidence and its false qualification flags remain unchanged.
