This protocol is prepared in response to the user's question about why the
overnight controller could not fix or restart after Joern Rust. The A26 stop
correctly rejected timing equivalence for Rust, but unnecessarily prevented
unrelated, unattempted commands from running. This changes future dispatch
control, not the acceptance requirements for any observation.

## Authorized untouched-queue continuation

The earlier question authorized preparation only. The user's subsequent
“I want you to resume / retry any unfinished efforts” authorizes this fresh
continuation. The earlier pending receipt is preserved; the launch plan instead
binds the new `authorized: true` receipt. Provenance, process ownership,
resources and quiet-host gates must pass before dispatch. No schedule or
recurring monitor is created.

The complete plan still contains 84 cold groups and eleven auxiliary commands.
Its 93 historically runnable commands are partitioned into 60 completed
candidate commands, the one preserved Rust mismatch, and 32 untouched commands.
The two Swift groups remain blocked and visible. The remaining dispatch order

The checkpoint verifies the original 55-command capture, the next five completed
commands, the Rust failure, and their source/report/raw evidence inventories.
It requires the 32-command suffix to have no analyzer-start or terminal output.
Old plans, failures, reports, qualification flags and raw observations stay
unchanged. New source and evidence paths are required when a launch is approved.

## Acceptance remains strict

Every cold group still undergoes the same frozen identity, configuration,
fixture, population and outcome comparison. A mismatch is never normalized,
accepted as equivalent, promoted into a baseline or counted as successful
qualification merely because the process exited zero.

For this explicit future mode only, a **complete, outcome-only comparison
mismatch** is retained as `semantic-mismatch-quarantined`. The process must
have exited zero without timeout or resource loss. All eight compared identity
fields and the complete case membership must match; every mismatch must be an
outcome difference. A partially compared or multiple-report group cannot use
this path. The group remains publication-ineligible, is not retried, and cannot
appear in completed-equivalent counts. The untouched queue then continues.

Identity, configuration, fixture or membership drift, invalid/missing evidence,
command failure, timeout, resource loss, and source/tool/runtime provenance
failures continue to stop dispatch without automatic retry. Warm and overhead
artifact validation is unchanged. Any inherited or new quarantine keeps the
overall result unqualified even if every remaining command finishes.

Joern Rust's original A26 terminal remains `failed`. Its 62 changed outcomes and
timings remain unqualified. Calling it inherited quarantine controls future
dispatch only; it does not rewrite that failure or prove the new Rust decisions
correct. Rust requires separate semantic/runtime qualification.

## Existing measurement and resource gates

All A25/A26 timing boundaries, pinned analyzer/query/model/runtime identities,
fixture populations, arguments, environments and per-command deadlines remain.
Cold, supported warm marginal, and invocation-overhead observations stay
separate. Missing, unsupported, inconclusive and runner-error states remain
visible; contended observations cannot become uncontended evidence.

Every future dispatch requires a fresh five-minute window at at least 90% CPU
idle, samples no more than 60 seconds apart, and no competing significant
workload. CPU contention or missing observations never interrupt an active
invocation. Commands run serially under the existing exclusive lock. The fresh
48-hour bound is checked between commands and while waiting, without extending
any expired parent clock or interrupting an active invocation.

The 127/125 GiB launch/continuous floors, 30 GiB cumulative source limit,
12 GiB per-source limit, and 24 GiB scratch/retention limits remain. Accounting
includes both prior campaigns and the new namespace, with identical scratch
paths counted once. The execution budget is exactly the sum of the 32 unchanged
deadlines. No unrelated workload is killed or worktree/evidence deleted.

Independent acceptance and publication review remain separate from capture.
Process-descendant containment and complete I/O attribution remain unproven.
No frozen release is rewritten and no cross-release speed comparison is made.
