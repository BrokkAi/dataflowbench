export const snapshot = snapshots.find((s) => s.version === 'v0.7.0');

Every result links its retained raw-evidence artifact (SARIF or analyzer
output), digest-bound in the
<a href={evidenceUrl(snapshot, snapshot.results.manifest.path)}>freeze manifest</a>.
That includes the runs that decide nothing: a `tool-native` scorecard whose
twelve results are all `unsupported` still retains its evidence and its
witnessed run identity.

Bifrost's, Semgrep CE's, Joern's, OpenTaint's, and the eleven CodeQL kernel
populations' artifacts here are **new bytes**, produced by this release's
re-runs. The eighteen carried-over reports keep the artifacts v0.6.1 retained,
carried into this manifest unchanged and re-verified against their recorded
digests. Nothing was re-run to make a number agree with another number.

This is the page the two amendments are actually decided on, because both of
them are claims about what the retained evidence *shows* rather than about what
a tool concluded.

- **The five withdrawn CodeQL negatives.** Open any of the
  `infeasible-branch-negative` cases for python, csharp, c, cpp, or ruby. The
  SARIF is empty of results, exactly as it was in v0.6.1 — and the endpoint
  probe evaluated beside the kernel query records the sink observed and the
  source **absent**, because the extractor pruned the constant-false branch
  before the source call ever became a data-flow node. A negative that rests on
  a source the analysis never had is not a decided negative, and under
  Amendment A30 it is no longer scored as one.
- **The three withdrawn Bifrost modeling negatives.** The
  `model-declared-source-negative` artifacts are unchanged in substance: they
  said, and still say, that the declared policy bound no endpoint. What changed
  is the normalizer, which now reads that diagnostic instead of scoring the
  empty result as a clean negative.
- **The four newly decided Bifrost cells.** The go `array-element` and php
  `map-iteration` artifacts no longer carry the `index_memory` value-flow
  diagnostic that made them decline under v0.10.8. That signature has not
  vanished from the freeze, though — it moved to the go `map-iteration` pair,
  which was and remains `inconclusive`. No outcome turns on it, and it is left
  visible here rather than tidied away.

Thirty-three slices are worth opening for the opposite reason. Semgrep CE,
Joern and OpenTaint all produced **new artifacts on new pins** whose normalized
outcomes are identical to v0.6.1's, case for case. The report digests differ,
the decisions do not.

Every invoked case in this freeze also retains a phase-timing sidecar beside
its raw evidence, and every run retains an environment stamp. Those are
additive metadata read only by the
[latency page](/snapshots/v0-7-0/latency/); no correctness
outcome on this page was derived from, or conditioned on, a timing value.
