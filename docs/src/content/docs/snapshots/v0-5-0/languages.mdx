Rows are (language, scorecard, tier) partitions — they share a table for
navigation, not a denominator. *Definitive* counts `reached` and
`not-reached` outcomes; *incomplete* counts `inconclusive`, `unsupported`,
and `runner-error`, which are reported as capability coverage and never
scored as negatives.

Three tiers appear here for java, javascript, and python: the `core` kernel,
the `modeling` matrix under the `benchmark-controlled` profile, and the
`modeling` probe set under the `tool-native` profile. They are separate
populations sharing a language name. Read the scorecard column, not just the
language.
