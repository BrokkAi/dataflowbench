import ScorecardSummary from '../../../../components/ScorecardSummary.astro';
import DimensionRates from '../../../../components/DimensionRates.astro';
import { snapshots } from '../../../../data/snapshots';

export const snapshot = snapshots.find((s) => s.version === 'v0.7.1');

Each scorecard is one frozen report: one analyzer, one track, one score
dimension, and one model profile. Scorecards are independent result
populations and are never combined into a leaderboard. `TPR`/`FPR` are
macro-averages over templates and then semantic dimensions; incomplete
outcomes are coverage, never negatives.

All **82 reports** in this snapshot were freshly rerun over the unchanged
852-case population. This includes the sixteen tool-native reports. The
freshness claim is evidence-backed: the <a href="https://github.com/BrokkAi/dataflowbench/blob/2007f15d687e0081c948e55bba39c952d248ee0f/reports/releases/v0.7.1/completed-measurement-audit.json">measurement audit</a>
records 82 fresh reports and 3,480 results.

The four changed cells are all in Java. Bifrost's `anonymous-implementation`
pair changed from `inconclusive` to `reached` / `not-reached`; OpenTaint's
`callback-registration` and `map-iteration` positive cells changed from
`not-reached` to `reached`. The per-scorecard tables below are the authority
for rates and denominators.

The two model profiles answer different questions. A
`benchmark-controlled` card measures an engine with the benchmark's model
supplied; a `tool-native` card measures the product with nothing supplied.
Native `unsupported` outcomes are declared capability boundaries and do not
mean two wrong answers.

{snapshot.results.scorecards.map((scorecard) => (
  <ScorecardSummary snapshot={snapshot} scorecard={scorecard} />
))}
