A snapshot is an immutable freeze. Corrected evidence never edits a published
snapshot: it creates a **new** one with a new release name and new digests, and
the superseded snapshot stays exactly as it was published, at the URL it was
published at. Nothing on this site is ever unpublished, and no page below has
been rewritten since its freeze — a citation of an archived page resolves
today and is meant to keep resolving.

The sidebar carries the two freezes most readers want — the current one and the
one before it. This page is the table of contents for everything older.

## Current and previous

- **<a href={`/snapshots/${current.slug}/`}>{current.version}</a> — current.**
  The stable pointer [`/current`](/current) always resolves here.
- **<a href={`/snapshots/${previous.slug}/`}>{previous.version}</a> — previous.**
  Superseded by {current.version}; its evidence and its numbers are unchanged.

Both are expanded in the sidebar, with their analyzers, languages, templates,
and case-evidence pages.

## Archive

Every entry below is a frozen release, newest first. The counts are read at
build time from that freeze's own archived results model, so they are the
numbers that snapshot published — not a later restatement of them. The freeze
ID is the SHA-256 of its manifest, and the evidence pin is the commit whose
tree holds that manifest and its retained evidence, permanently pinned so the
links keep resolving as `main` moves on.
