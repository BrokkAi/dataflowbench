Rows are `(language, scorecard, tier)` partitions. They share a table for
navigation, not a denominator. *Definitive* counts `reached` and `not-reached`;
*Inconclusive* preserves incomplete proof, *unsupported* records a declared
capability boundary, and *runner-error* records a failed execution. None is
scored as a negative. Language participation and model profiles remain
separate in the generated results.
